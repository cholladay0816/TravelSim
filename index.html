      TravelSim Madrid Help

      Move by double-clicking the map to plan a walking route (OSRM). Keyboard movement is disabled in this mode. Approach POIs (Airport, Hotel, Plaza Mayor, Museo del Prado, Retiro Park, Restaurant) to interact. When in range press Space or type and pick NPC to practice Spanish. Use Interpreter for English explanations. After completing all daily objectives, stand near the Hotel and press Sleep to advance the day.
